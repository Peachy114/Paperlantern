Best writer features for LanternPaper Novels

Yes—I researched current writing and serial-fiction platforms. The strongest approach is to combine:

Reedsy/Campfire-style writing and planning
Royal Road/Tapas-style serial publishing
Wattpad-style community interaction
Creator monetisation and analytics

Royal Road currently provides chapter drafts, volumes, bulk chapter importing, staggered releases, EPUB export, fiction-wide find-and-replace, autosaving, traffic sources, reader retention, follower history and chapter-level analytics.

Reedsy focuses on planning, drafting, editing, collaboration and professional book formatting, while Campfire adds character profiles, relationships, timelines, fictional calendars, maps, lore and manuscript version history.

Tapas supports scheduled episodes, tags, chapter comments and creator earnings through advertising and reader support.

For LanternPaper, build one creator platform with two main creation types:

Novels — text-based stories
Comics — manga, manhwa, manhua, webtoons and western comics

Do not create separate databases for manga, manhwa and manhua. They are all comics, but they can have different formats, reading directions and cultural origins.

Correct spelling: manhwa, not “manhuwa.”

Label	Common meaning
Manga	Comics originating from Japan
Manhwa	Comics originating from Korea
Manhua	Comics originating from China
Webtoon	A digital comic format, commonly using vertical scrolling
Western comic	Usually page-based and read left-to-right
Graphic novel	A longer page-based comic work

Manga is commonly associated with Japanese comics, while webtoons are strongly associated with Korean digital comics.

type WorkType =

    | 'hybrid'

Then store cultural or publishing classification separately:

type ComicClassification =

    | 'other'

This prevents problems such as assuming that every manga must use right-to-left pages or that every manhwa must use vertical scrolling.

Final recommendation

LanternPaper should use this central structure:

ONE CREATOR STUDIO
        │
        ├── Novel Editor
        │   ├── Rich text
        │   ├── Planning
        │   ├── Worldbuilding
        │   └── Chapter publishing
        │
        └── Comic Studio
            ├── Vertical scroll
            ├── Traditional pages
            ├── RTL / LTR reading
            ├── Image processing
            └── Episode publishing

The four highest-priority requirements are:

Creators must never lose their work.
Both vertical comics and traditional pages must work correctly.
Novel formatting must remain clean and readable on every device.
The same work should connect to novels, comics, art and commissions without being uploaded as unrelated projects.

That would position LanternPaper as more than another novel or webcomic website: it becomes a complete platform for creating, publishing, adapting and monetising story worlds.
